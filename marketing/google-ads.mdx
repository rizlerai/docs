**Google Ads** lets you connect a Google Ads login, manage campaigns and ads, check performance, and send Lead Form submissions into AI Sync as contacts.

<Note>
Open from **Marketing → Google Ads**, or **Settings → Integrations → Google Ads** → **Manage**.

This is **not** the same as Google Sign-In or Google Calendar. Connect Google Ads separately.
</Note>

## ✨ What you can do

- Connect Google Ads and sync ad accounts and Lead Forms
- Create a paused Search campaign, then **Pause** or **Resume** campaigns and ads
- Review **spend, impressions, clicks, leads**, and cost per lead
- Turn on lead delivery and send forms into **PowerLists**

## 📑 Tabs at a glance

| Tab | Use it to… |
|-----|------------|
| **Overview** | See connected account, ad accounts, and Lead Forms |
| **Ad Accounts** | Pick which ad account to work on |
| **Campaigns** | Create paused campaigns; pause or resume |
| **Ads** | Pause or resume individual ads |
| **Analytics** | View last-7-day results by campaign |
| **Lead Forms** | Webhook URL, Leads On, PowerList, map fields |

## 🔌 Connect Google Ads

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Go to **Marketing → Google Ads** (or **Settings → Integrations**) → **Connect Google Ads** → sign in and approve access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sync">
    Click **Sync Assets** so account names and Lead Forms fill in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select account">
    Choose an **Ad account** from the top dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up leads">
    Open **Lead Forms** → paste the **Webhook URL** in Google Ads → turn **Leads On** → **Map Fields**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Use a Google login with access to the Ads accounts you manage. A personal Gmail without Ads access may show an empty page.
</Tip>

### 🛠️ Top bar buttons

| Button | When to use |
|--------|-------------|
| **Ad account** | Switch which account you are managing |
| **Sync Assets** | Refresh accounts and forms from Google |
| **Reconnect** | Fix expired permissions |
| **Disconnect** | Unlink Google Ads from this workspace |

## 📣 Campaigns

Create and control campaigns for the selected ad account.

<img
  src="/images/marketing/google-ads/campaigns.png"
  alt="Google Ads Campaigns tab"
  className="rounded-lg border shadow-sm"
/>

| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Name** | Campaign name |
| **ID** | Google campaign ID |
| **Channel** | Type (for example Search) |
| **Status** | Active or Paused |
| **Actions** | Pause or Resume |

**Create a campaign**

1. Select ad account
2. Type a **Campaign name**
3. Click **Create paused**

<Warning>
New campaigns start **Paused**. Nothing spends until you click **Resume**.

This creates a paused **Search** campaign with a starter budget. Finish keywords, ads, and locations in Google Ads.
</Warning>

- **Pause** = stop spend fast
- **Resume** = may start delivery if ads, budget, and billing are ready

**Empty list?** Click **Sync Assets**. Create the form in Google Ads first if it still does not appear.

### 🔗 Webhook URL

The yellow box shows your **Webhook URL**. AI Sync does **not** attach it for you.

1. Copy the **Webhook URL** from this tab
2. Open that Lead Form in **Google Ads**
3. Paste the URL into the form’s webhook / lead delivery setting
4. Enter the **webhook key** your AI Sync admin provided

Do this on **each** form you care about.

### ✅ Turn leads on

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Leads On** | AI Sync should accept leads from that form (after the URL is pasted in Google Ads) |
| **Leads Off** | New leads from that form will not be stored here |

If no PowerList is assigned, contacts go to the company **Default** PowerList.

### 🗺️ Map Fields

1. Click **Map Fields** on the form
2. Match each Google question to an AI Sync contact field
3. Choose **skip** for questions you do not want imported
4. Click **Save mapping**

| Google question | Map to |
|-----------------|--------|
| Name / first / last | Name fields |
| Phone | Phone |
| Email | Email |
| City / State / Zip | Address fields |

<Warning>
Leads need: **Sync Assets** + **Webhook URL pasted in Google Ads** + **Leads On** + **field mapping**. Map phone and email for follow-up to work.
</Warning>
