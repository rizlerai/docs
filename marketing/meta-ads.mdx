**Meta Ads** lets you connect Facebook, manage campaigns and ads, check performance, and send Lead Form submissions into AI Sync as contacts.

<Note>
Open from **Marketing → Meta Ads**, or **Settings → Integrations → Facebook Lead Ads** → **Manage**.
</Note>

## ✨ What you can do

- Connect Facebook and sync ad accounts, Pages, and Lead Forms
- Pause or activate **campaigns** and **ads**
- Review **spend, clicks, leads**, and cost per lead
- Turn on lead delivery and send forms into **PowerLists**

## 📑 Tabs at a glance

| Tab | Use it to… |
|-----|------------|
| **Overview** | See connected account, businesses, and ad accounts |
| **Ad Accounts** | Pick which ad account to work on |
| **Campaigns** | Create, pause, or activate campaigns |
| **Ads** | Manage individual ads and creatives |
| **Analytics** | View spend and results by date range |
| **Pages & Lead Forms** | Turn leads on, assign PowerLists, map fields |

## 🔌 Connect Facebook

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Go to **Marketing → Meta Ads** (or [Integrations](/settings/social-sign-in-integration)) → **Connect Facebook** → sign in and approve permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sync">
    Click **Sync Assets** to pull ad accounts, Pages, and Lead Forms.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select account">
    Choose an **Ad Account** from the top dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on leads">
    Open **Pages & Lead Forms** → turn **Leads On** for your Page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Use a Facebook login with admin access to your Pages and ad accounts. A personal login without business access may show an empty page.
</Tip>

### 🛠️ Top bar buttons

| Button | When to use |
|--------|-------------|
| **Ad Account** | Switch which account you are managing |
| **Sync Assets** | Refresh data after changes in Facebook |
| **Reconnect** | Fix expired permissions |
| **Disconnect** | Unlink Facebook from this workspace |

## 📣 Campaigns

Create and control campaigns for the selected ad account.

| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Campaign** | Name and Facebook ID |
| **Objective** | Leads, Traffic, Sales, etc. |
| **Status** | Your switch — Active or Paused |
| **Effective Status** | What Facebook is actually doing |
| **Actions** | Rename, Pause, or Activate |

**Create a campaign**

1. Select ad account → **Create Campaign**
2. Enter name and choose objective
3. Click **Create Campaign**

<Warning>
New campaigns start **Paused**. Nothing spends until you click **Activate**.
</Warning>

- Click the **arrow** on a row to expand and view **Ad Sets**
- **Pause** = stop spend fast | **Activate** = confirm to start delivery

## 🖼️ Ads

Manage individual ads. Filter by campaign or ad set.

<img
  src="/images/marketing/meta-ads/ads.png"
  alt="Meta Ads Ads tab"
  className="rounded-lg border shadow-sm"
/>

| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Creative** | Ad image thumbnail |
| **Ad** | Ad name and ID |
| **Campaign / Ad Set** | Parent campaign and ad set |
| **Status** | Active or Paused (your switch) |
| **Effective Status** | Real delivery state (e.g. `CAMPAIGN_PAUSED`) |
| **Actions** | Edit, Pause, or Activate |

**Create an ad**

1. **Create Ad** → enter name
2. Pick **Campaign** → **Ad Set** → **Creative** (must exist in Facebook first)
3. Click **Create Ad**

<Warning>
New ads start **Paused**. Creatives are built in Facebook Ads Manager — AI Sync reuses them, it does not design them.
</Warning>

## 📄 Pages & Lead Forms

Turn Facebook form fills into contacts you can call or text.

<img
  src="/images/marketing/meta-ads/pages-lead-forms.png"
  alt="Meta Ads Pages and Lead Forms tab"
  className="rounded-lg border shadow-sm"
/>

| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Page** | Facebook Page name and ID |
| **Status** | **Leads On** or **Off** |
| **Forms** | Number of Lead Forms — click to open |

**For each form:**

1. Turn **Leads On** for the Page
2. Click **Forms** → assign a **PowerList**
3. Click **Map Fields** → match Facebook questions to contact fields → **Save Mapping**

| Facebook question | Map to |
|-------------------|--------|
| Full name | Name fields |
| Phone | Phone |
| Email | Email |
| City / State / Zip | Address fields |

<Warning>
Leads need all four: **Leads On** + **PowerList** + **field mapping** + Facebook connected. Map phone and email for follow-up to work.
</Warning>
