**AI Memory** keeps one short profile of each person, built from every text, email, call and team note:

- who they are and where things stand;
- what they want;
- their concerns;
- the agreed next step;
- facts they told you (budget, timeline, preferences).

Your **AI text agent** reads it before every reply, your **AI voice agents** receive it when a call starts, and your team sees it in the **Inbox** under the contact. Nobody asks the same question twice.

It is **off** until an account owner or admin turns it on.

![AI Memory](/images/ai-texting/9-memory-section.png)

## Turn it on

<Steps>
<Step title="Open AI Texting">
In the dialer or the main menu, open **AI Texting** and find **AI Memory**.
</Step>
<Step title="Switch it on">
Read what it does and the price, tick **I understand**, and click **Turn on**.

![Turn on AI Memory](/images/ai-texting/9-memory-confirm.png)
</Step>
</Steps>

Profiles are built after conversations (at most once every couple of minutes per person), so they stay current without you doing anything.

## Get the most out of it

- Turn on **Memory** on your AI voice agents too, so calls start with the profile.
- Keep notes on contacts. The profile reads them.
- Use the same calendar for voice and text agents.
- Read the profile in the Inbox before calling someone back.

## Export

Click **Export profiles (CSV)** at any time to download every profile as a spreadsheet.

## Turn it off

Switch it off at any time. New profiles stop being built and agents stop reading them. Existing profiles are kept and can still be exported.

## Costs

Each profile update is billed as **AI memory update** in your usage costs, at the **real AI cost plus 20%**: usually about a tenth of a cent, and never more than 0.5¢.
