When a lead calls one of your numbers, AI Sync decides **who** answers (a rep or your AI agent) and **where** it rings (in the dialer or on the rep's phone). Both are set in the dialer's settings panel, under **Inbound calls** and **Calls back to you**.

![Inbound calls and Calls back to you in the dialer](/images/dialer/incoming-call-routing/rail-settings.png)

## Who answers: Team or AI

Each of your numbers is set to **Team** or **AI**. Open **Inbound calls** in the dialer to see every number and change it.

![Each number set to Team or AI](/images/dialer/incoming-call-routing/numbers-team-ai.png)

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Team (recommended)" icon="users">
    Your reps ring first. If nobody picks up within 25 seconds, the number's AI agent answers. With no AI agent attached, the call goes to voicemail.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AI" icon="robot">
    The AI agent answers every call to this number. No rep rings.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
**AI** is greyed out on a number until an AI agent is attached to it. Attach one in the web app under **AI → Numbers**, then come back and choose AI or Team.
</Note>

### Send inbound calls to an AI agent

1. In the web app, open **AI → Numbers** and attach your inbound AI agent to the number.
2. In the dialer, open **Inbound calls** and find the number.
3. Choose **AI** to have the agent answer every call, or **Team** to ring your reps first with the agent as the backup.

<Tip>
Most teams choose **Team**: a person gets the first chance at a hot callback, and the AI agent makes sure nobody reaches a dead end.
</Tip>

## Which rep it rings

A lead who calls back rings the rep who **last had a real conversation with them**, as long as that rep is **Online and not already on a call**. If that rep can't take it, it rings the next free rep: reps assigned to the number, then reps on the lead's list, then the account owner.

Only a real conversation earns a callback. A rep who dialed the lead and got no answer doesn't take over that lead's callbacks.

<Note>
**Direct lines:** a number assigned to one rep as their own line rings only that rep. If they can't answer, it goes to that number's backup (voicemail, the AI agent, or the team), as set on the number.
</Note>

## Where it rings: in the dialer or on your phone

Each rep chooses under **Calls back to you**:

![Calls back to you](/images/dialer/incoming-call-routing/calls-back-to-you.png)

- **In the dialer:** the call pops up in the dialer with the lead's name and *They called you back*. Keep the dialer open in one browser tab.
- **On my phone:** the call forwards to your cell. Enter your personal cell the first time you choose it. Your company's own numbers can't be used, because they would ring themselves. Your phone shows the incoming number, not the lead's name.

You can also change this in the web app under **Profile → Personal Settings → Incoming Call**: [app.rizzdial.com/profile](https://app.rizzdial.com/profile). That page has two more options:

- **AI**: send every call meant for you to the AI agent.
- **Dialer + AI**: ring the dialer first, with the AI agent as the backup.

![Incoming Call field in Personal Settings](/images/dialer/incoming-call-routing/profile-personal.png)

## Quick answers

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I'm dialing. Will a callback reach me?">
    Only when you're free. A rep already on a call is skipped, so the lead rings the next free rep, then the number's backup.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a callback go straight to my phone?">
    Yes. Choose **On my phone** under **Calls back to you** and enter your cell.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can the AI take every inbound call?">
    Yes. Attach an AI agent to the number in **AI → Numbers**, then set the number to **AI** under **Inbound calls**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
