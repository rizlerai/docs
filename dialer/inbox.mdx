The **Inbox** tab in the dialer is where reps read and answer replies. There's
one row per contact, never one row per message, so the list stays short no
matter how much you text.

```text
┌ Inbox ─────────────┬ Conversation ─────────────────┬ Contact ─────────┐
│ 🔍 Search          │  Jordan Lee          [Call]   │  Phone           │
│ All  Unread  Mine  │            Today              │  Email           │
│ ● Maria Gomez 5:46 │   Hi, you're booked! ▶        │  Tags            │
│   Sam Patel   Sun  │ ◀ Yes Thursday at 3 works     │  Open full       │
│   +1 928…     Sat  │  [Text | Email]  From: line   │  contact ↗       │
└────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────┴──────────────────┘
```

## 🧭 Find the conversation you need

- **Search** by name, phone number or email.
- **All** shows every conversation, newest first.
- **Unread** shows conversations where the lead's reply is the latest message
  and nobody has opened it yet (last 30 days).
- **Mine** shows conversations you've texted or that were routed to you.
- A **blue dot** marks an unread conversation. The number on the **Inbox** tab
  counts them.
- Opening a conversation marks it read. **Mark all read** (the double tick)
  clears the whole list for everyone, so use it with care.

New replies appear live, with no refresh needed.

## 💬 Reply without leaving the dialer

Click a contact to open the **whole conversation**: texts, emails and calls
together, grouped by day.

- **Text** sends from the lead's texting line. The line is shown next to the
  box ("From your line …" or "From team line …"). See
  [Texting lines](/dialer/texting-lines) for how it's chosen.
- **Email** sends from your company email. It's greyed out when the contact has
  no email address.
- **Enter** sends. **Shift + Enter** adds a new line.
- **Call** rings the lead from the dialer.
- **Open full contact** opens their complete record in a new tab.

<Note>
**Email replies don't come into the Inbox yet.** When a lead answers an email,
the reply goes to **the email address of the rep who sent it**. For
automations, that's the rep who saved the disposition, else the account
owner. Companies with their own email sender keep their own reply address.
Emails you send are shown in the conversation.
</Note>

## 📞 Calls, recordings and transcripts

Every call with the lead sits in the conversation at the time it happened:

- **How it ended:** *Call completed*, *No answer*, *Voicemail*, *Busy*, or *AI agent call*, with the length and the rep.
- **Play** the recording right there.
- **Show transcript** to read what was said. AI agent calls also show their summary.

You hear and read your own calls. The account owner, and anyone allowed to
see all contacts, hears every call. Everyone else still sees that a call
happened and how it ended.

<Note>
The transcript appears a few seconds after the call ends, and the recording
shortly after. Click the refresh arrow at the top of the list if a call you
just finished isn't there yet.
</Note>

## 📱 On a phone or small screen

The list shows first. Tap a contact to open the conversation, and use the back
arrow to return. The contact card is hidden on narrower screens; **Open full
contact** is always a click away.

## 🙋 Who sees what

Each reply is routed to one rep. It shows under their **Mine** filter, and they
get the notification. Everyone with Inbox access can see **All**. See
[Where replies go](/dialer/texting-lines#-where-replies-go).

## 🧯 If something is off

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="A lead replied but it's not in the Inbox">
The number they replied to isn't sending replies to AI Sync. Run the
[two-minute test](/dialer/texting-lines#-two-minute-test-do-this-for-every-new-client).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="&quot;No texting line set up&quot;">
There's no A2P-linked number to text from. See
[What counts as a texting line](/dialer/texting-lines#-what-counts-as-a-texting-line).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The unread count doesn't match what I see">
The count covers the last 30 days, and a conversation stops being unread when
anyone opens it or you reply. Refresh with the circular arrow at the top of the
list.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
